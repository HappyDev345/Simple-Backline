# 🎬 BACKLINE: The Challenges Ahead
**Duration:** 2:30 minutes
**Tone:** Professional, honest, inspiring
**Style:** Screen recording with text overlays + B-roll footage of AV work (lighting, sound) or code snippets

---

### **[0:00 - 0:45] INTRODUCTION**
*(Visual: Screen starts with the Backline landing page in action. Fast-paced montage of user interactions—clicks, uploads, scrolling feed)*
**Voiceover:**
"Every major live production, every concert, festival, and broadcast, starts with one thing: a team of people. Sound engineers, lighting designers, camera operators. They work together behind the
scenes.

But right now? There's no good place to connect them. Most social networks are too big, or too noisy. Backline is different. It’s built for the people who *actually* make live shows happen. We’re
building a dedicated network just for AV professionals."

*(Visual: Cut to code snippet of `App.jsx` routing)*
**Voiceover:**
"The tech behind this? Modern. We're using React for the frontend, Supabase for secure, real-time data management, and Vercel for fast global deployment. But it's not about just showing off
technology."

---

### **[0:45 - 1:30] CHALLENGE 1: DATA & SECURITY**
*(Visual: Switch to Supabase connection logs or Database schema diagrams)*
**Voiceover:**
"First challenge? Trust and Data. AV professionals share gear, setups, and project files—some sensitive, some proprietary. If a leak happens, it's expensive.

That’s why we’re being careful. Every profile, every uploaded video, every forum post is guarded by our Supabase architecture. We designed the database schema so that only those who’ve signed in can
trust the data they see—and that private files stay secure. Security isn’t an afterthought; it’s built into every table."

*(Visual: Show the `AuthModal` code from `App.jsx`)*
**Voiceover:**
"Authentication needs to be seamless but secure. We’re ensuring that when a technician logs in, their network stays visible, and their projects stay protected. That balance between open collaboration
and security is hard—but necessary."

---

### **[1:30 - 2:15] CHALLENGE 2: COMMUNITY GROWTH**
*(Visual: Show the forum section in the HTML file)*
**Voiceover:**
"Second challenge? Growth. We can’t just build a tool and expect people to show up. AV professionals are busy. They’ve got shows on weekends, jobs on days, and tech support calls.

That’s why Backline is designed for speed. Mobile-first. The forum isn't a distraction; it's part of the workflow. If you’re looking at crew profiles on your phone during transit, we need it to load
instantly. That’s why we’re optimizing every Vercel edge route, and ensuring our Supabase real-time subscriptions don’t slow down when thousands of users try to connect simultaneously."

*(Visual: Show the `feed` page or the crew card layout)*
**Voiceover:**
"We also need to make sure the community is *right*. No generic social media. Just crews, gear, and shows. If we can get that right, it becomes a vital part of their day-to-day work."

### **🎥 Visual Notes & Tips for Production:**
- **Screenshots:** Use your `index.html` file as a background in the intro and outro to establish brand identity.
- **Code B-roll:** When talking about Supabase/Vercel, insert short overlays showing the code from `App.jsx` (routing section) or database queries. This proves the technical foundation.
- **Tone:** Keep it professional but human. It’s not just “tech,” it’s people connecting.
